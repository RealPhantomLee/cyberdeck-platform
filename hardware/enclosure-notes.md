# 🧳 Enclosure Notes  
Brick 2 — Cyberdeck

This document captures enclosure and physical layout decisions.

It does not prescribe a single enclosure solution.

---

## Enclosure Goals

- Protect hardware during transport
- Allow airflow and heat dissipation
- Provide access to ports and power
- Support future expansion
- Maintain portability

---

## Considered Options

### Off-the-Shelf Case
**Pros**
- Fast
- Durable
- Minimal design effort

**Cons**
- Limited customization
- May restrict airflow or port access

---

### DIY / Custom Enclosure
**Pros**
- Fully customizable
- Cyberdeck aesthetic
- Expansion-friendly

**Cons**
- Time investment
- Higher complexity

---

### Vehicle-Mounted Variant (Future)
- Requires vibration resistance
- Heat management is critical
- Power source must be isolated
- Not required for Brick 2 completion

---

## Layout Considerations

- Pi should be easily removable
- Storage should be secured
- Battery must not press against boards
- Fans should not blow directly onto battery cells

---

## Notes

- Enclosure choice may change without affecting Brick 2 scope
- Major enclosure redesigns should be logged in `CHANGELOG.md`
